# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

Calendar Focus Sync is a free, open-source macOS app. This policy describes what the app does with your information. The short version: everything stays on your Mac, and nothing is collected.

## What the app accesses

- **Calendar events.** With your permission, the app reads events from the calendars you choose in order to know when to turn a Focus Mode on and off. Event details are used only on your Mac, in memory, to schedule those actions.
- **Shortcuts.** The app installs and runs a Shortcut named `calendar-focus-sync`, because macOS only lets apps control Focus Modes through Shortcuts.
- **Notifications.** If you enable them, the app shows a local notification shortly before a Focus Mode starts.
- **Preferences.** Your settings (selected calendars, lead time, notification choice) are stored locally using the standard macOS user defaults.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.
- It does not contain analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- It does not use cookies or any third-party services, and it makes no network connections.

## Permissions

The app runs inside the macOS App Sandbox and requests only the Calendars permission. You can revoke it at any time in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars**; the app then stops syncing until access is granted again.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published in this repository together with the date above.
